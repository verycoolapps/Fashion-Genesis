## 🇬🇧 English

### What It Is

Fashion Genesis X is an AI skill package that **invents original fashion products** — clothing, footwear, bags, accessories, smart wearables, and entirely new categories that don't exist yet. It is not a design assistant that arranges silhouettes; it is an innovation engine that destroys assumptions, fuses disciplines, mutates functions, and ships scored, audited, manufacturable inventions.

Every output is forced through five quality gates — anti-slop rejection, originality audit, human-centered safety, a 100-point score, and a 3-variant creative evolution — so what comes out is engineered, not generated.

### The Impossible Idea Mutation Engine

Run on **every** invention. This is the creative heart — six stages that turn a brief into a breakthrough:

```
① ASSUMPTION DESTRUCTION   → hidden conventions exposed;
                             "what if this assumption were unnecessary?"
② CROSS-INDUSTRY FUSION    → aerospace, robotics, biomimicry, naval
                             architecture, neuroscience, sculpture, math
③ FUNCTIONAL MUTATION      → what fundamentally different job
                             could this product do?
④ AESTHETIC MUTATION       → silhouette, proportion, texture, volume,
                             geometry
⑤ EXPERIENCE MUTATION      → how it opens, transforms, moves,
                             adapts, stores
⑥ INVENTION SYNTHESIS      → fuse the strongest findings; REJECT combos
                             that are visually interesting but
                             functionally empty
```

Cross-industry fusion must be *meaningful* — the foreign discipline must solve a real product problem or unlock a real capability. Random mashups die at the Anti-Slop Gate.

### The 5 Quality Gates

Every invention is forced through all five — no exceptions:

| Gate | What it kills / enforces |
|---|---|
| 🚫 **1. Anti-AI-Slop Check** | Rejects random attachments, gratuitous spikes, generic cyberpunk, needless glow, copied designer signatures, reskinned silhouettes. *Strange ≠ innovative. Gold ≠ premium. Glow ≠ futuristic.* |
| 🔍 **2. Originality Audit** | Closest products, similar mechanisms, patent analogs, what's genuinely different. Labeled **PRELIMINARY** when research is unavailable — never fake citations. |
| 🧍 **3. Human-Centered & Responsible Design** | Ergonomics, skin-contact safety, toxicity, fire/electrical/battery safety, accessibility, body diversity, cultural sensitivity, repairability. *Reject what can't be responsibly mitigated.* |
| 📊 **4. 100-Point Quality Score** | Originality 20 · Creative Concept 15 · Functional Innovation 15 · Aesthetic Excellence 15 · UX 10 · Technical Feasibility 10 · Material Innovation 5 · Commercial Potential 5 · Sustainability 5. Bands: 95+ exceptional · 90–94 world-class · 85–89 strong · 75–84 needs work · <75 rework. *Never inflate scores.* |
| 🧬 **5. Autonomous Creative Evolution** | After drafting: **A Refined** (preserve identity, improve), **B Radical** (attack architecture assumptions), **C Unexpected** (interdisciplinary wildcard). Ship the strongest. |

### The Originality Contract

Originality is **built**, never asserted. The skill targets Level 4–5 on its own ladder:

| Level | Classification |
|---|---|
| 1 | Cosmetic Variation |
| 2 | Design Differentiation |
| 3 | Functional Innovation |
| 4 | Conceptual Breakthrough |
| 5 | Category Creation |

Every serious invention runs the Mandatory Originality Audit. If research is unavailable, the audit is labeled **PRELIMINARY** — the skill never fabricates citations, patents, prices, or certifications.

### Capability Scope

**Categories covered:** clothing (all types), footwear, bags & carrying systems, accessories (wearable/fashion/tech), fashion extensions (eyewear, headwear, jewelry, luggage, wearable art, fashion-furniture hybrids) — and **entirely new product categories** that don't exist yet. The skill never restricts itself to existing product types.

### Collection Mode

Ask for a whole collection and the skill outputs: name, manifesto, central inspiration, philosophy, audience, visual identity, color palette, material palette, signature construction language, product categories, individual concepts, hero product, supporting products, styling direction, editorial campaign, and commercial positioning — every item sharing recognizable DNA.

### How It Thinks

- **Lead with creation, not interrogation.** One sharp clarifying question max — then invent.
- **Conversational, energetic, precise.** Vague brief → intelligent assumptions → creation.
- **Bilingual natively.** English and Indonesian, matched to your language and energy.
- **Honest about limits.** Never claims a runtime capability that doesn't exist.
- **After delivery, offers:** evolution variants, deeper spec, collection expansion, image generation, commercialization pass.

### The A–M Master Output Format

Every serious invention ships all thirteen sections:

```
A. INVENTION REVEAL        — name, ID, category, innovation level, originality score
B. THE BIG IDEA            — concept + creative philosophy
C. WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT — vs. conventional products, genuine innovation
D. VISUAL DESIGN           — silhouette, views, surface, signature details
E. MATERIALS               — primary/secondary/structural + rationale + flags
F. FUNCTIONAL MECHANISM    — how it works, transforms, adjusts
G. TECHNICAL SPEC          — dimensions, components, targets (estimates labeled)
H. USER EXPERIENCE         — comfort, fit, movement, maintenance, real use
I. MANUFACTURING STRATEGY  — prototype path vs. mass production, complexity, QC
J. VISUAL GENERATION       — image prompts (10 views) or generated visuals
K. MARKET POTENTIAL        — customer, positioning, differentiation
L. ORIGINALITY AUDIT       — similarities, research gaps, IP notes
M. FINAL CREATIVE VERDICT  — what's special, what to improve, prototype first
```
