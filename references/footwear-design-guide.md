# Footwear Design Guide

Reference module for Fashion Genesis X. Covers last shapes, sole architectures, cushioning systems, upper construction, fastening mechanisms, biomechanics, modular concepts, and adaptive/orthopedic footwear.

Status convention: **[VERIFIED]** = commercial/established, **[EXPERIMENTAL]** = demonstrated in labs or limited production, **[SPECULATIVE]** = conceptual or early research.

---

## 1. Footwear Anatomy & Terminology

### 1.1 Basic Parts

- **Upper:** Everything above the sole — covers foot, provides fit and protection.
- **Sole:** Bottom part that contacts ground — midsole, outsole, insole.
- **Last:** 3D form around which shoe is built — determines fit and shape.
- **Insole:** Removable or fixed inner sole — provides cushioning and comfort.
- **Midsole:** Between insole and outsole — primary cushioning.
- **Outsole:** Bottom layer — provides traction and durability.
- **Heel counter:** Rigid piece stabilizing heel.
- **Toe box:** Front part covering toes.
- **Vamp:** Front/top part of upper covering instep.
- **Quarter:** Back/sides of upper covering heel and ankle.
- **Collar:** Padded rim around ankle opening.
- **Tongue:** Padded flap under laces.
- **Eyelets/eyelet stays:** Holes and reinforcements for laces.
- **Laces/fastenings:** Closure system.
- **Pull tab:** Loop for putting on shoe.
- **Heel:** Raised back portion.
- **Toe spring:** Upward curve at toe for natural roll-off.
- **Rocker:** Curved sole geometry for smooth gait.
- **Shank:** Rigid piece in midsole for torsional support.
- **Counter:** Rigid heel support.
- **Quarter lining:** Interior lining of upper.
- **Sock liner:** Moisture-wicking interior layer.

## 2. Last Shapes

### 2.1 What is a Last?

A last is the 3D foot-shaped form used to build shoes. It determines:
- Fit (width, volume, shape)
- Toe shape (round, pointed, square)
- Heel height and position
- Arch support
- Overall proportion

### 2.2 Last Types

| Last Type | Shape | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Round toe** | Curved, natural | Sneakers, boots, casual |
| **Pointed toe** | Tapered, elongated | Formal, dress shoes, some boots |
| **Square toe** | Flat front | Work boots, some casual |
| **Almond toe** | Rounded point | Heels, dress shoes |
| **Peaked toe** | Upturned point | Oxfords, some dress shoes |
| **Duck bill toe** | Wide, flat | Climbing shoes, some casual |
| **Pointe toe** | Extremely pointed | Ballet pointe shoes |
| **Open toe** | Exposed toes | Sandals, some heels |
| **Mule/toe** | Varies | Mules, slides |

### 2.3 Last Dimensions

- **Length:** Foot length + allowance (typically 3-10mm).
- **Width:** Foot width at ball + ease.
- **Volume:** Height/depth of foot.
- **Heel height:** 0mm (flat) to 150mm+ (platform).
- **Toe spring:** Upward curve at toe.
- **Rocker:** Curvature of sole for roll-off.
- **Arch:** Support structure.
- **Ball width:** Width at widest point.
- **Heel width:** Width at heel.

### 2.4 Last Materials

- **Wood:** Traditional, durable, heavy.
- **Plastic:** Modern, lightweight, customizable.
- **Composite:** Carbon fiber, fiberglass — light, strong.
- **3D-printed:** Custom, precise, experimental.

### 5.3 BOA System Deep Dive

**How it works:**
- Dial on side of shoe
- Rotating dial pulls thin steel cables through lace guides
- Micro-adjustable, one-handed operation
- Instant release by pulling dial outward

**Advantages:**
- Precise fit adjustment
- Quick on/off
- No loose laces
- Works with gloves

**Disadvantages:**
- Expensive
- Mechanical failure points
- Limited to specific brands

**Applications:** Snowboard boots, ski boots, some athletic shoes, some hiking boots.

**Status:** [VERIFIED] — commercially successful, expanding.

## 6. Biomechanics & Gait Analysis

### 6.1 Gait Cycle

**Phases:**
1. **Heel strike:** Initial contact, heel hits ground.
2. **Foot flat:** Entire foot on ground.
3. **Midstance:** Body weight over foot.
4. **Heel off:** Heel lifts, toe pushes off.
5. **Toe off:** Toes push off ground.
6. **Swing:** Foot moves forward.

**Key metrics:**
- **Stride length:** Distance between steps.
- **Cadence:** Steps per minute.
- **Ground contact time:** Time foot on ground.
- **Vertical oscillation:** Up/down movement.
- **Pronation:** Inward roll of foot.
- **Supination:** Outward roll of foot.
- **Overpronation:** Excessive inward roll.
- **Underpronation (supination):** Insufficient inward roll.

### 6.2 Pronation Types

| Type | Description | Shoe needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Neutral** | Normal inward roll | Neutral shoes |
| **Overpronation** | Excessive inward roll | Stability/motion control shoes |
| **Underpronation (supination)** | Insufficient inward roll | Cushioned shoes |
| **Severe overpronation** | Extreme inward roll | Motion control shoes, orthotics |

### 6.3 Biomechanical Alignment

- **Neutral alignment:** Knee tracks over second toe.
- **Knee valgus (knock-knee):** Knee inward.
- **Knee varus (bow-legged):** Knee outward.
- **Leg length discrepancy:** One leg shorter.
- **Flat feet (pes planus):** Collapsed arch.
- **High arches (pes cavus):** Excessive arch.
- **Hallux valgus (bunion):** Big toe deviation.
- **Hammertoe:** Toe contracture.
- **Plantar fasciitis:** Heel/arch pain.
- **Achilles tendonitis:** Achilles inflammation.
- **Shin splints:** Tibial stress.

### 6.4 Gait Analysis Methods

| Method | Description | Applications |
|---|---|---|
| **Visual assessment** | Watch person walk | Basic screening |
| **Pressure mapping** | Measure pressure distribution | Orthotics, shoe design |
| **Motion capture** | 3D movement tracking | Research, performance |
| **Force plates** | Measure ground reaction forces | Research, performance |
| **EMG** | Muscle activity | Research, rehabilitation |
| **In-shoe sensors** | Pressure/temperature sensors | Smart footwear |
| **Video analysis** | Frame-by-frame review | Coaching, rehabilitation |
| **AI gait analysis** | Machine learning from video/sensors | Consumer apps, clinical |

### 6.5 Shoe Design for Biomechanics

| Goal | Design response |
|---|---|
| **Cushioning** | Soft midsole, gel, air units |
| **Stability** | Medial post, guide rails, firm midsole |
| **Motion control** | Maximum support, orthotic-friendly |
| **Flexibility** | Soft upper, flexible sole |
| **Durability** | Durable outsole, reinforced upper |
| **Lightweight** | Lightweight materials, minimal padding |
| **Breathability** | Mesh, knit, perforations |
| **Waterproofing** | Membranes, sealed seams |
| **Traction** | Rubber outsoles, lug patterns |
| **Energy return** | Foam technology, carbon plates |

## 8. Adaptive & Orthopedic Footwear

### 8.1 Adaptive Footwear

**What:** Footwear designed for specific needs, disabilities, or medical conditions.

**Features:**
- **Easy on/off:** No laces, magnetic closures, wide openings.
- **Adjustable fit:** Velcro, elastic, adjustable straps.
- **Support:** Arch support, cushioning, stability.
- **Protection:** Reinforced toe, ankle support.
- **Breathability:** Mesh, ventilation.
- **Lightweight:** Easy to walk in.

**Examples:**
- **Nike FlyEase:** One-handed entry, auto-lacing.
- **Under Armour Playmakers:** Adaptive design for kids.
- **SAS (San Antonio Shoemakers):** Orthopedic shoes.
- **Drew Shoe:** Orthopedic, comfortable.
- **Orthofeet:** Diabetic-friendly, orthopedic.
- **New Balance 990/993:** Extra width options.
- **Vionic:** Orthotic sandals.

### 8.2 Orthopedic Footwear

**Conditions addressed:**
- **Diabetes:** Prevent ulcers, protect feet.
- **Arthritis:** Reduce pain, support joints.
- **Plantar fasciitis:** Heel/arch support.
- **Bunions:** Wide toe box, adjustable fit.
- **Flat feet:** Arch support.
- **High arches:** Cushioning, support.
- **Leg length discrepancy:** Adjustable heel.
- **Edema:** Extra width, adjustable fit.
- **Neuropathy:** Protective, cushioned.
- **Post-surgery:** Protection, support.

**Design features:**
- **Extra depth:** Room for orthotics, swelling.
- **Removable insoles:** Custom orthotics.
- **Wide toe box:** Prevent pressure.
- **Adjustable closures:** Velcro, laces, buckles.
- **Cushioned midsole:** Shock absorption.
- **Rock-solid heel counter:** Stability.
- **Seamless interior:** Prevent irritation.
- **Breathable materials:** Prevent moisture.

### 8.3 Custom Orthotics

**Types:**
- **Prefabricated:** Off-the-shelf, adjustable.
- **Custom-molded:** Made from foot impression.
- **3D-printed:** Personalized, precise.
- **Electronic:** Sensor-integrated.

**Applications:**
- Correcting biomechanical issues.
- Reducing pain.
- Preventing injury.
- Improving performance.
